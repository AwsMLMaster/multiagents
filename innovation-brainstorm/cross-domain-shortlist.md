# Cross-Domain "1+1" Shortlist — Inventions Whose Parts Already Exist

**The pattern:** every idea below is a fusion of two-or-more mature, published,
shipping knowledge bases that *no one has multiplied together yet*. None require a
scientific breakthrough — only that someone build the bridge between siloed
communities. Each entry gives the fusion, the gap, why it's feasible, the honest
risk, and the cheapest decisive test.

Date: 2026-06-11

These are deliberately kept short — idea-stage, not full proposals. The three
deeper write-ups in this folder (glymphatic delivery, Grid Radar, ReCharge
cartridges) show what any one of these looks like fully developed.

---

## 1. Mycelium + Mycorrhizal Networks → Living Self-Healing Building Insulation
**Fuse:** fungal materials science (mycelium composites, already sold as packaging
and acoustic panels) **+** soil microbiology (mycorrhizal nutrient-transport
networks) **+** building science.
**The 1+1:** Mycelium is already grown into rigid insulation panels — but they're
*killed and dried* before use. Nobody has built insulation that stays **alive and
dormant**, reactivating with a trace of moisture and nutrient to **regrow into and
seal cracks, gaps, and air leaks** itself over a building's life.
**Why feasible:** mycelium-growth, dormancy/revival, and panel manufacturing are
all demonstrated. The unbuilt part is the dormancy-controlled, self-healing
formulation in a building envelope.
**Risk:** keeping it dormant-but-revivable without rot or allergen/spore release;
fire code. **Cheapest test:** wall panels with seeded gaps in a humidity chamber —
does dormant mycelium revive and seal on cue without spreading uncontrolled?

## 2. Acoustic Levitation + Pharmaceutical Crystallization → Container-less Drug Manufacturing
**Fuse:** acoustic/ultrasonic levitation (lab-proven, levitates droplets) **+**
crystallization chemistry (crystal form determines a drug's efficacy and is
notoriously hard to control) **+** process automation.
**The 1+1:** Drug crystal *polymorph* (which form crystallizes) makes or breaks a
medicine, and container walls seed unwanted forms. Levitate the droplet so it
touches nothing, and you can **grow pure, single-polymorph crystals with no
container contamination** — nobody has turned acoustic levitation into a
production crystallization platform.
**Why feasible:** both halves are demonstrated separately; the merge is
engineering throughput, not new physics.
**Risk:** scaling beyond microliter droplets to useful yields. **Cheapest test:**
crystallize a known polymorph-finicky drug levitated vs. in a vessel — is the
levitated product purer/more controllable?

## 3. Cheap MEMS Inertial Sensors + Seismology → Crowd-Sourced Street-Level Earthquake Early Warning
**Fuse:** the accelerometers already in every phone/car/EV **+** seismology
(P-wave-before-S-wave physics) **+** distributed-sensing ML.
**The 1+1:** National early-warning systems exist but rely on sparse, expensive
seismometers. Millions of parked EVs and phones are idle accelerometers. Fuse them
into a **block-by-block** warning grid that also maps *which specific buildings*
shook hardest (instant damage triage). Phone-quake projects exist but nobody has
fused the dense EV fleet + structural-response mapping.
**Why feasible:** sensors are deployed; the math is known; edge compute is cheap.
**Risk:** noise rejection and incentivizing fleet participation. **Cheapest test:**
instrument a fleet in a seismically active city; can fused signals beat the
official network's warning time and add per-building shake maps?

## 4. Electrochromic Glass + Agrivoltaics → Spectrum-Splitting Greenhouse Skin
**Fuse:** tunable electrochromic/photonic coatings (smart windows) **+** plant
photobiology (plants use mostly red/blue light; green is wasted) **+** solar PV.
**The 1+1:** Greenhouses either grow plants *or* harvest solar — a tradeoff. A
**spectrum-splitting** skin passes the red/blue light plants need and diverts the
rest (green/IR) to PV — so the same roof feeds the crop *and* generates power, with
electrochromic tuning per crop and season. Static spectral-splitting films exist;
nobody has made a *tunable, integrated greenhouse envelope* of it.
**Why feasible:** spectral-selective coatings, agrivoltaics, and electrochromics
all exist. **Risk:** coating cost and durability under greenhouse conditions.
**Cheapest test:** split-skin vs. normal greenhouse panel — measure crop yield AND
kWh from the same area.

## 5. Doppler Wi-Fi/UWB Sensing + Sleep Medicine → Contactless Whole-Home Vital-Sign Monitor
**Fuse:** RF sensing (Wi-Fi/UWB radar detects breathing and heartbeat through walls
— demonstrated) **+** sleep & cardiology clinical knowledge **+** anomaly-detection
ML.
**The 1+1:** Wearables monitor *one person who remembers to wear them.* The Wi-Fi
already saturating every home can passively sense **respiration, heart rate, falls,
and sleep apnea for everyone in the house with zero devices on the body.** RF
vital-sensing is published; a turnkey *clinical-grade whole-home* product fusing it
with sleep/cardiac diagnostics isn't built.
**Why feasible:** the RF physics and ML are demonstrated; routers are everywhere.
**Risk:** multi-person separation, clinical validation, and (huge) privacy/consent
design. **Cheapest test:** validate RF-derived respiration/apnea events against
gold-standard polysomnography in real bedrooms.

## 6. Enzyme Engineering + Textile Recycling → Home/Curbside Polyester Depolymerization
**Fuse:** engineered PET-digesting enzymes (proven to depolymerize plastic) **+**
textile science **+** distributed reverse-logistics.
**The 1+1:** Enzymatic PET recycling is being industrialized for *bottles*, in big
central plants. Clothing is ~60% polyester and almost never recycled because it's
mixed-fiber and dispersed. A **standardized enzymatic drop-bin/appliance** that
depolymerizes polyester textiles back to monomer near the point of disposal —
turning the dispersed-waste problem into feedstock — hasn't been built.
**Why feasible:** the enzymes and the bottle-recycling process exist; this is
applying them to textiles in a distributed format. **Risk:** handling fiber blends,
dyes, and enzyme cost. **Cheapest test:** run real mixed-textile waste through the
enzyme process — what monomer yield and purity, at what cost?

## 7. Solid-State Cooling (Electrocaloric) + Refrigerated Logistics → Self-Powered Cold-Chain Labels
**Fuse:** electrocaloric/thermoelectric thin films (solid-state heat pumping, no
compressor) **+** printed flexible electronics + energy harvesting **+** cold-chain
data science.
**The 1+1:** Vaccines and food spoil silently in transit; today's "cold-chain
monitors" only *record* temperature, they don't *fix* it. A **thin, flexible patch**
that both senses AND actively spot-cools a small payload using solid-state cooling
powered by harvested/printed energy — a self-defending cold patch — hasn't been
made.
**Why feasible:** electrocaloric films, flexible electronics, and harvesting all
exist. **Risk:** cooling power vs. patch size/energy budget. **Cheapest test:** can a
patch-scale electrocaloric film hold a few cm³ below threshold through a realistic
heat excursion?

## 8 (struck). Large Language Models + Formal Verification → Self-Proving Code for Critical Systems
**Fuse:** LLM code generation **+** formal methods / theorem provers (which can
*mathematically prove* a program correct but are too laborious for humans to use
widely) **+** software engineering.
**The 1+1:** LLMs write code fast but can't guarantee it's correct; formal
verification guarantees correctness but is too slow for humans to author. Pair them:
the **LLM writes both the code and the machine-checkable proof, and the prover acts
as ground-truth referee** in a loop — making formally-verified software cheap for
the first time. Pieces exist in research; a usable verified-by-construction
toolchain for everyday critical code isn't productized.
**Why feasible:** capable code LLMs and mature provers both exist. **Risk:** proof
generation is still hard for complex specs; spec-writing UX. **Cheapest test:**
target a narrow critical domain (e.g., parsers, crypto primitives) — can the loop
produce proven-correct implementations faster than experts?

## 9. CRISPR Diagnostics + Municipal Wastewater + Genomics → City-Scale Pathogen Weather Map
**Fuse:** CRISPR-based nucleic-acid detection (cheap, field-deployable) **+**
wastewater epidemiology (proven at scale during COVID) **+** genomic surveillance +
data science.
**The 1+1:** Wastewater surveillance proved it can see outbreaks early; CRISPR
diagnostics are cheap and multiplexable. Fuse them into **always-on sewer sensors
across a city** producing a live, neighborhood-resolution "**pathogen weather
map**" — flu, COVID, norovirus, antibiotic-resistance genes — before clinics see
patients. Each piece is deployed; the integrated continuous network isn't.
**Why feasible:** both halves ran at scale already. **Risk:** sensor fouling,
sensitivity, and governance. **Cheapest test:** instrument a handful of sewer nodes
in one city; does the CRISPR-sensor signal lead clinical case data?

## 10. Piezoelectric Roads + EV Inductive Charging → Self-Powered Dynamic Charging Lanes
**Fuse:** piezoelectric energy harvesting (roads generate power from traffic
weight/vibration) **+** inductive (wireless) EV charging **+** grid power
electronics.
**The 1+1:** Both piezo road-harvesting and in-road wireless EV charging are
demonstrated *separately.* Combine them: a lane that **harvests energy from the
weight of passing vehicles and feeds it straight into the in-road chargers** — a
partly self-powered electric road that reduces grid draw. Nobody has closed that
local harvest-to-charge loop.
**Why feasible:** both technologies exist in pilots. **Risk:** piezo yield is
modest vs. charging demand — likely a supplement, not full self-sufficiency.
**Cheapest test:** a short instrumented road segment — what fraction of charging
energy can local harvesting actually supply?

## The selection lens (how these were chosen, and how to find more)

Each had to pass four filters:
1. **Both halves already exist** and are published/shipping — *no breakthrough
   required.*
2. **The combination is genuinely unbuilt** — not just unscaled, but not assembled
   at all in this form.
3. **The barrier is organizational/conceptual** (siloed communities, business-model
   inertia) rather than physical — the best kind of barrier.
4. **A cheap, decisive experiment exists** that can validate or kill the core
   hypothesis before any large commitment.

If I had to rank by *nearest-term, lowest-risk*: **#3 (crowd-sourced quake
warning)** and **#4 (spectrum-splitting greenhouse)** are buildable now with
obvious buyers. By *largest upside*: **#8 (self-proving code)** and **#9 (pathogen
weather map)**. By *cleanest consumer-commodity fit*: **#1 (living insulation)** and
**#5 (contactless home vitals)**.
